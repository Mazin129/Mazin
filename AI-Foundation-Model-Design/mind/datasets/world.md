Geography is the study of the Earth's surface, its features, and its inhabitants.
The Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known place with life.
The Earth is about 71 percent covered by water.
A continent is one of the Earth's seven large landmasses.
The seven continents are Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, and Australia.
An ocean is a very large body of salt water, and the Earth has five oceans.
The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth.
A river is a large natural stream of water that flows toward an ocean, lake, or another river.
The Nile is the longest river in the world, at about 6,650 kilometres.
The Amazon is the largest river in the world by the volume of water it carries.
A mountain is a landform that rises high above the surrounding land.
Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level on Earth.
The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world.
A desert is a region that receives very little rain.
The equator is the imaginary line around the middle of the Earth.
A climate is the typical weather of a region over a long period of time.
An earthquake is a sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement in the Earth's crust.
A volcano is an opening in the Earth's crust through which lava and gases erupt.

Astronomy is the study of stars, planets, and everything in outer space.
The Solar System is the Sun and everything that orbits it.
The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System and it provides light and heat to the Earth.
The Solar System has eight planets orbiting the Sun.
The eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.
Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System.
Mercury is the smallest planet and the closest to the Sun.
The Moon is the Earth's only natural satellite.
The Moon causes the ocean tides through its gravitational pull.
A star is a huge ball of hot gas that produces light and heat through nuclear fusion.
A galaxy is a vast collection of billions of stars held together by gravity.
The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System.
A comet is a ball of ice and dust that orbits the Sun and grows a tail when it comes near.
An asteroid is a small rocky body that orbits the Sun.
A black hole is a region of space where gravity is so strong that not even light can escape.
A light-year is the distance light travels in one year.

The human body is made of many organs that work together to keep a person alive.
The skeleton is the framework of bones that supports and protects the body.
The human skeleton has 206 bones in adults.
A muscle is tissue that contracts to produce movement.
Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to every cell and removes waste.
The stomach is the organ that breaks down food using acid.
The kidneys are the organs that filter waste from the blood to make urine.
The skin is the largest organ of the body and it protects everything inside.
The nervous system carries signals between the brain and the rest of the body.
Vitamins are nutrients the body needs in small amounts to stay healthy.
Exercise strengthens the heart and improves overall health.
