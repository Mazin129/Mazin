Physics is the science of matter, energy, and the forces that govern the universe.
Energy is the capacity to do work and it cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed.
Force is a push or a pull that can change the motion of an object.
Gravity is the force that pulls objects with mass toward each other.
Gravity causes objects to fall toward the ground when they are dropped.
Friction is the force that resists motion between two surfaces in contact.
Friction causes moving objects to slow down and produces heat.
Mass is the amount of matter in an object and it stays the same everywhere.
Weight is the force of gravity acting on an object's mass and it changes with location.
Velocity is the speed of an object in a specific direction.
Acceleration is the rate at which an object's velocity changes over time.
Heat is energy that flows from a hotter object to a colder object.
Electricity is the flow of electric charge, usually carried by electrons.
A conductor is a material that lets electric current flow through it easily.
An insulator is a material that resists the flow of electric current.
Magnetism is a force produced by moving electric charges that attracts or repels certain materials.
Light is electromagnetic radiation that the human eye can see.
The speed of light in a vacuum is about 300,000 kilometres per second and nothing can travel faster.
Sound is a vibration that travels through a medium such as air, water, or solid material.

Chemistry is the science of matter and how substances combine and change.
An atom is the basic building block of matter.
An atom has a nucleus of protons and neutrons surrounded by electrons.
A proton carries a positive charge and a neutron has no charge.
An electron carries a negative charge and orbits the nucleus.
An element is a pure substance made of only one kind of atom.
A molecule is two or more atoms bonded together.
A chemical reaction rearranges atoms to form new substances.
Water is a molecule made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.
An acid is a substance that releases hydrogen ions and has a pH below 7.
A base is a substance that accepts hydrogen ions and has a pH above 7.
Matter exists mainly in three states: solid, liquid, and gas.
Heating a solid causes it to melt into a liquid.
Heating a liquid causes it to evaporate into a gas.

Biology is the science of living things.
A cell is the basic unit of life and every living thing is made of one or more cells.
DNA is the molecule that carries the genetic instructions for building and running a living thing.
A gene is a section of DNA that codes for a specific trait or protein.
Mitochondria are the parts of a cell that turn food and oxygen into usable energy.
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight to make food from carbon dioxide and water.
Photosynthesis produces the oxygen that most living things need to breathe.
Evolution is the process by which living things change over many generations through natural selection.
The heart is the organ that pumps blood around the body.
The lungs are the organs that take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide.
The brain is the organ that controls the body and is the center of thought and memory.
Bacteria are tiny single-celled organisms, and some cause disease while many are harmless or helpful.
A virus is a tiny infectious agent that can only reproduce inside a living cell.
